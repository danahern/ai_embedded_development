Board Version
This application note is applicable to the Ensemble E8 DevKit (DK-E8) Rev. A or Rev. B

Device Supplies




            Supply Name              Description
            VDD_USB_3V3              Supply for USB PHY, 3.3V only
            VDD_BUCK                 Supply for internal DC-DC
            VDD_MAIN                 Supply for internal LDOs
            VDD_IO_1V8               Supply for majority of IO pins, 1.8V only
            VDD_IO_FLEX              Supply for subset of IO pins, 1.8V or 3.3V
            VDD_BATT                 Supply for internal LDOs



Power Measurement Points
The DevKit has no shunt resistors so an ammeter or DMM with ammeter mode is required.
JP1 allows you to measure VDD_USB_3V3. This supply is only relevant if the USB PHY is to be used,
otherwise it can be left floating or tied to ground to reduce power.
JP2 allows you to measure VDD_BUCK, VDD_MAIN, and VDD_BATT.
JP3 allows you to measure VDD_IO_1V8.
JP4 allows you to measure and configure VDD_IO_FLEX for 1.8V or 3.3V mode.
JP5 should be kept in position 1-2 so that VDD_BATT is tied to BUCK and MAIN on JP2.

Most power will be drawn through JP2 (BUCK+MAIN+BATT). This is the primary measuring point when
evaluating the Ensemble MCU’s power modes.
During heavy IO operations, there will be power drawn through JP3 (VDD_IO_1V8).

Board Modifications
The DK-E8 Rev. B does not have JP1 through JP4 installed in the factory, the jumpers are bypassed with
cuttable shunts as shown in the schematic. Before measuring power on the DevKit through these
jumpers, it is necessary to cut the traces on S4 through S7.
At a minimum: Only cut S5 and measure current through JP2 (BUCK+MAIN+BATT).
Optionally: Cut S6 and connect a second ammeter to JP3 (VDD_IO_1V8).
When using USB: Cut S4 and measure USB PHY current through JP1 (VDD_USB_3V3).
